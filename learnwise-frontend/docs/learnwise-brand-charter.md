# Learnwise Visual Charter

## Brand idea

Learnwise is a curious learning studio: clear enough to trust, expressive enough to make discovery feel rewarding. The identity should feel energetic without becoming noisy.

## Core palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | Deep cobalt navy | `#17205C` | Headings, navigation, primary text |
| Signal | Electric cobalt | `#2848D8` | Links, active states, key surfaces |
| Spark | Coral pink | `#FF4F87` | Small emphasis, success moments, logo accent |
| Air | Cyan | `#57D8FF` | Secondary highlights, diagrams, focus support |
| Grow | Acid lime | `#D8FF45` | Primary calls to action, progress, positive state |
| Paper | Cool white | `#FBFCFF` | Page background |
| Mist | Soft blue | `#EEF2FF` | Panels, tabs, quiet surfaces |

Use Ink and Signal for structure. Use Spark, Air, and Grow as accents, never as a full-page rainbow. Text must remain readable without color alone.

## Typography

- Display and headings: Manrope, 700-800
- Body and interface: DM Sans, 400-700
- Accessible fallback: Arial, system-ui, sans-serif
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs. Use uppercase only for short labels.
- Keep body text at least 16px on public pages and line-height around 1.5-1.8.

## Logo direction

Create a compact mark built from two intersecting paths that form a `+` or connected learning node. The mark should suggest connection and a moment of insight, not a graduation cap, book, lightbulb, or brain icon.

Recommended lockup:

`[coral connection mark] learnwise`

Use the wordmark in Ink on light backgrounds and white on cobalt backgrounds. Keep the mark legible at 24px. Do not use gradients, shadows, mascots, or generic education symbols.

## Logo-generation prompt

> Design a premium, modern logo for “learnwise”, an AI-powered learning studio that connects courses, questions, practice, voice learning, and progress. Create a minimal geometric connection mark made from two intersecting paths forming a subtle plus sign and an “aha” junction. Use deep cobalt navy `#17205C`, coral pink `#FF4F87`, electric cobalt `#2848D8`, and acid lime `#D8FF45`. Pair it with a clean lowercase wordmark in a geometric sans-serif similar to Manrope. Flat vector style, strong silhouette, accessible contrast, no graduation cap, no book, no lightbulb, no brain icon, no gradient, no mockup, transparent background, horizontal and icon-only variants.

## Accessibility rules

- Never communicate state with color alone; add text, icon, or shape.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus with a high-contrast outline.
- Respect reduced motion and provide calm mode.
- Keep controls large enough for touch and spaced apart.
- Provide captions/transcripts for audio and never make sound the only path.
- Keep the layout predictable, with clear headings and one primary action per section.
